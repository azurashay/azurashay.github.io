01 / Problem

Sales weren't coming back

The platform captured engagement per account, but reps rarely came back to read it. We needed a view a rep could grasp in seconds.

02 / Insight

Engagement is a two-way story

What matters isn't a pile of views - it's how the lead responds to the rep's outreach.

03 / Decision

One glanceable timeline

A reason to log back in

Folloze · Conversational AI · 2026

The Campaign Agent

A conversational AI that turns a few plain sentences about a product into a personalized B2B campaign.

Designing the wait. Generation takes real seconds, so the waiting experience is a designed surface, not a spinner.

01 / Problem

The blank-canvas tax

Starting an ABM campaign meant four decisions at once - product, audience, message, goal. Easy to abandon.

02 / Insight

Talking beats form-filling

People describe what they sell in plain words more easily than they fill out forms. So let the conversation do the structuring.

03 / Decision

An agent that asks, then assembles

A chat that asks one question at a time, with a live panel mirroring the progress, ending in a ready-to-launch board.

Measured ~28%

From idea to board in minutes

Trading the blank canvas for a guided conversation lifted campaign-page creation by roughly 28%, and cut the time to prepare a page from hours to minutes.

Under the hood: prompt engineering

The simple chat hides two layers of prompt engineering.

01 · Section prompts

Every block knows itself

Each section carries its own content and purpose, so the agent knows what it's there to do.

02 · Page-builder agent

Assembles the page

A second prompt takes everything the user gave and decides which sections to pull and how to compose them into a page.

Dell EMC · Enterprise UX · 2017

The XtremIO Dashboard

A storage-management dashboard that showed every metric to every user. I turned it into three focused views, one per audience.

01 / Problem

One screen, everyone, everything

The original dashboard threw health, performance, capacity, top volumes and weekly patterns at every user with equal priority.

02 / Insight

It wasn't one audience - it was three

Session recordings and work with the PM surfaced three distinct groups. Each one went to a single metric first and came to the rest later.

03 / Decision

Three tabs, three priorities

I split the dashboard into three tabs, each owning one group's key metric - with a summary strip up top so no one lost the wider picture.

Observed

Each group went straight to its own tab

Users settled into the tab built for them and navigated outward from there. Read from session recordings, not instrumentation.

Folloze · Campaign analytics

The Impact Dashboard

Board-level analytics that turn a running campaign into one readable story: who showed up, what they engaged with, and which channels brought them in.

01 / Problem

Impact was invisible

Campaigns generated plenty of engagement. Proving it meant stitching numbers together from boards, accounts and channels by hand, after the fact.

02 / Insight

The story lives at the campaign level

People, accounts, content and sources only mean something together - one level above pageviews, one level below the quarterly report.

03 / Decision

Headlines first, drill-downs behind

Five KPIs against the previous period carry the headline. Everything else lives one click deeper, so the screen stays calm.

Not measured

The whole campaign, at a glance

One screen to see what a campaign did, and every headline number opens into the proof behind it. It shipped; there is no post-release data behind it.

Theme Builder for Customer Success

A live tool that lets our Customer Success team spin up branded mobile themes without touching a designer. I designed it and I built it.

01 / Problem

A designer in every loop

Every branded mobile theme went through a designer, for work that was configuration rather than design. It was a queue, and the queue was me.

02 / Insight

The decisions were already bounded

Fonts, colours, radii - the choices were a finite set with rules attached. Something that constrained can be handed over safely if the tool holds the rules.

03 / Decision

Give them the tool, keep the rules

A live builder that only produces themes that are already on-brand, so Customer Success ships them directly.

In use

No designer in the loop

Customer Success ships branded themes on their own. Usage is not instrumented, so there is no adoption number behind that.
