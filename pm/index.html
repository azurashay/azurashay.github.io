The Designer - Folloze's page builder

Product owner · 2019 to present

Every ABM campaign at Folloze is built here. Marketers assemble product pages and resource centres by adding sections and setting toggles that control what each section shows. I owned the area, not only its design.

Where work came from

Customer Success brought what customers were missing - sections and controls that didn't exist yet.

How it was decided

Gainsight PX settled it: session recordings, heatmaps and flow drop-off showed which parts were actually failing.

How it shipped

Prioritised backlog in ClickUp, specs written, a dedicated developer on a bi-weekly cadence, and I QA'd every release.

After launch

A help article for every feature, plus in-app announcements so users found what was new.

Campaign Agent - conversational campaign builder

Product + design

Starting a campaign meant a dozen decisions at once - product, audience, message, goal. Heavy up front, easy to abandon. We replaced the blank canvas with a conversation that asks one question at a time and assembles the campaign as it goes.

The bet

People describe what they sell far more easily than they fill in forms. Let the conversation do the structuring.

The hard part

Two layers of prompt architecture: every section carries its own purpose, and a builder agent composes them into a page.

Designing the wait

Generation takes real seconds, so the waiting state was a designed surface rather than a spinner.

Outcome

Page creation up ~28%, and prep time down from hours to minutes.
